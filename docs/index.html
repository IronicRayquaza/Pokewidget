PokéWidget

Animated Pokémon sprites, living on your home screen.

Any Pokémon, in any game's art. Tap one to hear its cry. Android only, and free.

Installing it

This is not on the Play Store, so Android will check twice that you meant it. Both warnings are normal and neither means anything is wrong:

  1. Open the downloaded file. Android asks whether your browser is allowed to install apps. Say yes — you only do this once.
  2. Play Protect says it does not recognise the app. It says that about everything that did not come from the Play Store. Tap Install anyway, or More details first if that button is hidden.
  3. Add a widget. Long-press an empty part of your home screen, tap Widgets, find PokéWidget, and drag one out.

What it does

  • Every Pokémon, in whichever game's art you like — Emerald, Diamond and Pearl, HeartGold and SoulSilver, Black and White, Scarlet and Violet, and the animated Showdown set.
  • Sprites really animate on the home screen, with no background service and no battery cost.
  • Tap one to hear its cry. Shiny, female and back sprites wherever the games had them.
  • Castform and friends change form with the real weather, in a city you choose.
  • Once a sprite has downloaded once, it keeps working with no connection at all.
